Capabilities · 01

Designed for mathematical intuition

Created for students, researchers, and the mathematically curious to experience the geometry hidden inside complex functions first-hand.

01 / PHASE & MAGNITUDE

Domain colouring in 3D

Terrain elevation maps directly to modulus \( |f(s)| \) while surface hue encodes the argument \( \arg(f(s)) \), augmented by crisp contour isolines.

02 / UNBOUNDED DOMAIN

Endless procedural horizon

No bounding boxes or static meshes. The complex plane unfolds continuously around you as you walk, sprint, or fly across any region.

03 / ROOT FINDING

Newton & critical walks

Trigger automated Newton-step descent to converge directly onto nearby zeros \( f(s)=0 \), or lock onto the critical line \( \operatorname{Re}(s)=\tfrac{1}{2} \).

04 / RIEMANN ZETA

Analytic continuation

Explore \( \zeta(s) \), Dirichlet \( \eta(s) \), \( \beta(s) \), and \( \Gamma(s) \) across the entire plane using reflection formulas and Borwein acceleration.

05 / RIEMANN SURFACES

Immersive branch portals

Traverse between sheets of multivalued functions—such as \( \log(z) \), \( z^{1/n} \), and inverse trig functions—by walking through branch-cut portals.

06 / LIVE INSTRUMENTATION

Instant parameter morphing

Edit function parameters, zoom factor, series iterations, and lighting time-of-day on the fly and watch the landscape respond immediately.

Instrumentation · 02

Precision telemetry while you explore

The in-game HUD tracks every coordinate, function evaluation, and detected root in real time without obscuring the horizon.

Zeros Registry

Automatically detects and catalogues distinct zeros \( f(s)=0 \) encountered during your walk, displaying their exact real and imaginary coordinates.

Position & Phase Dial

Live readout of Re and Im components for both \( s \) and \( f(s) \), paired with an angular \( \arg(f) \) compass and modulus \( |f| \).

Domain Minimap

Overhead 2D domain-colouring radar showing your camera frustum, contour lines, and discovered roots across the surrounding complex plane.

The Mathematics · 03

From rational polynomials to the Riemann Hypothesis

The explorer supports a rich library of complex functions—including trigonometric, exponential, rational, logarithmic, and fractal families—each evaluated directly in custom GPU shaders and high-precision C++ routines.

Special emphasis is placed on the Riemann zeta function \( \zeta(s) \), combining the Dirichlet eta series, Riemann's functional reflection equation, and Borwein's accelerated algorithm to render the critical strip seamlessly:

\[ \zeta(s) = 2^s \pi^{s-1} \sin\!\left(\frac{\pi s}{2}\right) \Gamma(1-s)\,\zeta(1-s) \]

Dive into the full mathematical breakdown, Lanczos Gamma coefficients, and shader precision notes in the technical documentation.
